One line of plain text: the excuse a gamer texts their boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week — for the [Make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-the-excuse-that-almost-works-69fde8f5) bounty ($2, top 8).

## The entry

> Jury service summons arrived — I'm in court from 9am tomorrow and they won't let me defer it.

Why this one: a jury summons is the one excuse a tired manager can't argue with — a legal summons, a fixed 9am start, and deferral genuinely hard to get. Nobody can verify it quickly, and the airtightness itself is the joke: "they won't let me defer it" is the weary tell of someone who already tried. Boring, specific, checkable-sounding, no dead grandmother, no flat tyre, no sudden fever — and no mention of GTA, gaming, or the launch.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry byte-identical to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), guarded to the contest gate: 1–140 characters and at most 20 words. Run it with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
